Questions

Before you write

Do you take small jobs?

Yes, if the work is considered — a single room, a stair, a wall that has to last. We do not take volume fit-outs.

Do we need an architect first?

Not always. We can start from a brief and bring a drawing set, or work under an architect you already have.

How long to a first visit?

Usually within two weeks of a clear note about the site. We will say if we are not the right contractor.

Where do you work?

Bengaluru first. Farther jobs by appointment when the brief is right.
